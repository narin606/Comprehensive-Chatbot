Oneberry Technologies is a Singapore-based physical security automation expert founded in 2003 by Ken Pereira. The company focuses on solving manpower constraints and operational inefficiencies by blending security manpower with automation. Its solutions emphasise proactive monitoring, streamlined processes, and measurable productivity gains for customers in government, commercial, and residential sectors.

Innovation is core to Oneberry's culture. The in-house R&D team continually develops AI, video analytics, robotics, and IoT capabilities that raise security industry standards. These technologies support command centre operations, field deployments, and hybrid human-machine workflows.

The company vision is to be the number one provider of security automation. Mission statements highlight continuous innovation, staying close to client pain points, and remaining nimble to market changes. Collaborative partnerships allow Oneberry to extend offerings into adjacent domains such as facilities management and smart city initiatives.

Oneberry runs programmes to upskill the workforce, supports outcome-based contracts, and maintains 24/7 operational readiness through its command centre. Case studies and testimonials describe improvements in remote guarding, video surveillance uptime, and customer experience.
