Oneberry offers a portfolio of technology-driven solutions that align with the categories presented on the corporate website. Each item combines automation with security expertise to boost efficiency and resilience for public- and private-sector clients.

Anomaly Detection AI
- Computer-vision analytics (including ARVAS) that spot unusual behaviours across CCTV networks so security teams can respond proactively instead of manually reviewing footage.

Virtual Guard
- Remote guarding service delivered from the Oneberry Command Centre, pairing CCTV, access control, concierge interactions, and outcome-based service levels to reduce manpower needs.

Security Services
- Integrated manpower and managed services that complement technology deployments, providing site operations, incident response, and compliance coverage.

Mobile Surveillance
- Rapid-deployment camera systems and trailers that extend monitoring coverage to temporary or hard-to-wire locations with secure connectivity.

Video Analytics
- AI modules that enhance live and recorded video feeds with event detection, search, and automation features tailored to customer workflows.

Cyber Defence
- Solutions such as Invisiron that harden networks with stealth intrusion prevention, autonomous threat blocking, and compliance reporting for enterprises and SMEs.

Clean Energy
- Power systems (e.g., EFOY smart fuel cells and hybrid energy packs) that keep remote surveillance and communications equipment online with minimal maintenance.

Security Robots
- Customisable patrol and concierge robots that assist human officers with routine tasks, visitor engagement, and data collection; adoption is supported by local grant schemes.

Productivity Software
- Command-centre and operations software that unifies device telemetry, workforce management, and reporting to raise productivity and service quality.

Defence
- Advanced sensors, communications, and ruggedised platforms that support homeland and defence applications requiring resilience and rapid response.

Bluetooth Technology
- Secure Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) solutions for indoor positioning, access control, and asset tracking that integrate with other Oneberry platforms.
